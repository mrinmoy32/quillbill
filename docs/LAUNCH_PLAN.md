# Getting your first 10 paying customers

## The honest picture
Invoice tools are crowded (Zoho, Vyapar, Refrens, Wave and many free generators). A generic launch won't work. v1's wedge is **simplicity for people who don't use any invoicing tool yet** (they use Word, Excel or WhatsApp). Payment reminders is the paid upgrade story.

## Before writing more code (this week)
1. Pick **one** niche in India and write it down. Candidates: freelancers/designers, tutors and coaching classes, small contractors/electricians, home bakers and boutiques selling on Instagram, clinics. Choose the group you can reach in person or through a community you're already in.
2. Talk to 10 of them. Ask: "How do you create invoices today? How do you chase late payments? What did the last late payment cost you?" Don't pitch yet.
3. Offer a **founding member** deal: ₹X for a year (or a lifetime discount) for the first 10, paid upfront or via the first Razorpay subscription. A person who pays has validated the idea; a person who says "nice" hasn't.

## Pricing starting point (test it, don't trust it)
Free: 3 invoices/month with a small "created with" footer (this is your free marketing: every invoice sent shows your name). Pro: ₹199-399/month or ₹1,999-2,999/year. Charge from day one.

## Onboarding script for each customer
Sit with them (call/screen share) for their first invoice. Time it. Write down every moment they hesitate. Those notes are your roadmap, ahead of any feature idea.

## Distribution that fits a solo founder
- Niche communities: WhatsApp/Telegram groups, subreddits like r/IndiaBusiness or r/freelanceIndia, LinkedIn posts about a problem (late payments), not about your product.
- A free single-purpose page that ranks: "GST invoice generator for freelancers" or "invoice template for tutors"; it converts visitors to sign-ups.
- Build in public (short posts with real numbers).
- Referral nudge: the footer on every free invoice.

## Metrics to watch weekly
Sign-ups, % who create an invoice within 24h (activation), % who send it, invoices per active user, free→Pro conversion, and why people stop. Ten paying customers beat a thousand sign-ups.

## What to build after v1 (in this order, validated by customers)
1. Payment reminders (your stated USP): scheduled emails/WhatsApp for overdue invoices. 2. UPI/payment link on invoices. 3. GST split (CGST/SGST/IGST) and HSN/SAC codes if your niche needs them. 4. Recurring invoices. 5. Meeting booking.
